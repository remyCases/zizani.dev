## Stoneshard, the reason I got so deep into modding

During 2023, I discover that neat indy game named [`Stoneshard`](https://stoneshard.com/). I instantly clicked with the art style. Even though it was in early access (I try not to buy early access games), I bought it and spent an ungodly amount of hours in this game.

However, it is a difficult game, and I was frustrated by the gameplay duration. Since it was still at its debut, there was no real quest nor lore to follow, just make a character and play with the mechanics. But recreating a new character was painfully slow and I had no incentive to replay the same four quests other and other.

So, I started using CheatEngine to move faster, and try other spells and skills in the same character. Then, I ask myself, how difficult would it be to mod the game itself ?
